Support for Gemini Copilot

Need help setting up or using Gemini Copilot for PowerPoint? Follow the instructions below.

1. Obtaining a Google AI Studio API Key

  1. Visit Google AI Studio.
  2. Sign in with your Google account.
  3. Click Create API Key and copy the generated key.
  4. In the PowerPoint Add-in, click the ⚙️ Settings icon, paste the key, and click Test Connection.

2. Common Troubleshooting

  • Quota or Rate Limit Error (429): Verify that your Google Cloud account has billing enabled if your free tier quota has been reached.
  • Invalid API Key: Ensure there are no leading or trailing spaces in the key string.
  • Slide Not Detected: Click the Scan button in the add-in to refresh the PowerPoint slide reading context.
